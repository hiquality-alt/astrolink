HOW TO PLAY

Each dive has seven prompts. You have 25 seconds to enter one answer for each prompt.

Answers must be in the game's answer bank. If your answer is not accepted, try another one before time runs out.

Accepted answers earn points based on their assigned rarity. Every point adds 10 metres to your dive.

Daily Dive is one attempt per day. Unlimited lets you practise whenever you like.
